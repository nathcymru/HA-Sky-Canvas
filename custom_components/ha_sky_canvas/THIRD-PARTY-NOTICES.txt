Met Office published forecast symbols and forecast colours
Retrieved: 28 October 2026

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
Licence v3.0.
Licence: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/
Website terms: https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/policies/tandc

The distribution includes the 30 original, unchanged weather-condition SVGs
published in the Met Office forecast guide (codes 0-30 except unused code 4).
Source: https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/guides/what-does-this-forecast-mean
Original URL pattern:
https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/api/images/file/{code}svg?prefix=images

Each original source URL, file size and SHA-256 digest is recorded in
frontend/weather-icons/v1/manifest.json. Original gradients, view boxes and
embedded PNG shadows are preserved. These files are served locally.

Temperature and UV background colours were transcribed from the official
forecast page's inline styles:
https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/forecast/gcpvj0v07
Their selectors, exact values, retrieved HTML digest and presentation
adaptations are recorded in frontend/weather-icons/v1/palette-source.json.
The application selects black or white numeric text for contrast; it does
not reproduce Met Office typography. Numeric values and category labels
accompany the colours. Full source notes are in
frontend/weather-icons/v1/SOURCE.md.

The Met Office public website terms permit public content reuse under OGL
v3 subject to their exclusions. No separate ownership or licence statement
was found in the inspected guide or symbol SVGs. These assets and colour
values retain their source terms and are excluded from the application's
MIT licence. No Met Office logos, fonts, site scripts or tracking are copied.
The application is independent and is not endorsed by the Met Office.
Weather-data attribution is separate: Powered by Met Office data.
